# Memory: Game Rules & Logic

## 1. Dodis Godis Spelet (Front Side)
- Players roll a 6-sided die to advance their pawn along the 16-square circular candy track.
- Each square triggers a challenge: trivia, truth/dare, mini-duel, or taking a sip.
- Players pick a candy piece from the central bag when landing on bonus spots.
- The game concludes when the bag is empty; the player with the final candy wins.

## 2. Swedish Yatzy (Back Side)
- Traditional 5-dice Swedish rules.
- 3 rolls per turn with hold toggles.
- Categories: Ones through Sixes, Bonus (63+ points gives +50 bonus), One Pair, Two Pairs, Three of a Kind, Four of a Kind, Full House, Small Straight (1-2-3-4-5), Large Straight (2-3-4-5-6), Chance, Yatzy (50 points).

## 3. Date & Party Cards
- 4 card categories:
  - Icebreaker (easy conversation starters)
  - Fun (party humor & dares)
  - Deep (intimate & reflective)
  - Naughty (18+ drinking challenges, marked with shot mode indicators)
