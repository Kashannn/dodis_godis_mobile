# Memory: Game Rules & Logic

## 1. Dodis Godis Spelet (Main Game & Outer Track)
- Players roll a die to advance their pawn/container clockwise along the outer track.
- Landing on a square triggers a duel/challenge with a chosen rival player.
- Winning the challenge claims the candy piece from that spot!
- Game concludes when the final candy is taken; the player claiming the last candy wins!

### The 9 Official Track Challenges:
1. **Drop Challenge (Droppa)**: Drop a candy from chin height, closest to the target mark wins.
2. **True or False (Sant eller Falskt)**: Active player makes a statement; opponent guesses true/false.
3. **Closest Toss (Kast Närmast Vinner)**: Toss a die/candy towards a target; closest to bulls-eye wins.
4. **Coin Flip (Singla Slant)**: Flip a coin; opponent calls Crown (Heads) or Clover (Tails).
5. **Guess the Hand (Gissa Hand)**: Hide candy in one fist; opponent guesses which hand.
6. **Catch with Mouth (Fånga Godis med Munnen)**: Toss candy in the air and catch it directly in mouth.
7. **Pull the String (Dra Sticka)**: Pull sticks with hidden strings; longest string wins.
8. **Spin the Top (Snurra)**: Spin tops or candies simultaneously; longest duration wins.
9. **Super Sour Face (Supersur)**: Suck on super sour candy for 15s without making a face/grimace.

## 2. Swedish Yatzy (Back Side)
- Traditional 5-dice Swedish rules.
- 3 rolls per turn with hold toggles.
- Categories: Ones through Sixes, Bonus (63+ points gives +50 bonus), One Pair, Two Pairs, Three of a Kind, Four of a Kind, Full House, Small Straight (1-2-3-4-5), Large Straight (2-3-4-5-6), Chance, Yatzy (50 points).
